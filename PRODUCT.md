# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Collie is used by security leaders, IT managers, operations teams, and business owners at Australian and APAC organisations. They are setting up phishing simulations, importing employees, launching training campaigns, and checking whether security awareness is improving without creating shame or anxiety for staff.

## Product Purpose

Collie is a modern phishing simulation and security awareness platform. It helps organisations run realistic campaigns, track clicks and reports, deliver just-in-time microlearning, and explain risk scores clearly. Success means an admin can onboard, import employees, launch a seeded campaign, and see training outcomes quickly while every employee interaction feels constructive.

## Brand Personality

Calm, capable, plainspoken. Collie should feel modern and polished, closer to Linear or Vercel than legacy security software, but without performative minimalism. The voice is direct and encouraging: train, don't trick. Avoid shaming language, gotcha patterns, corporate filler, and alarmist red screens.

## Anti-references

Avoid enterprise security ware from 2014, open-source admin clutter, dark hacker aesthetics, humiliation-driven training flows, generic SaaS hero-card patterns, and any interface that treats employees as targets. Red is reserved for critical alerts, not ordinary learning feedback.

## Design Principles

1. Make the next operational step obvious.
2. Treat every missed cue as a teachable moment.
3. Keep tenant boundaries and safety states visible where they matter.
4. Use Australian language and context throughout.
5. Earn trust through restrained polish, transparent data, and calm copy.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus states, reduced motion, responsive layouts for tablet use, and colour choices that do not rely on hue alone. Copy should be understandable under stress and avoid shame, blame, or surprise.
